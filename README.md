## Analytics Approach

My projects follow an end-to-end analytical workflow:

**Business Problem → Data Preparation → Analysis → Validation → Dashboard Development → Insight Generation → Recommendations**

I focus not only on reporting what happened, but also on understanding **why it happened, where performance gaps exist, what risks or opportunities the data reveals, and what decision-makers should do next**.

Where appropriate, I incorporate AI into this workflow to accelerate repetitive analytical tasks, support hypothesis generation, improve documentation, and increase the speed of analysis without replacing analytical judgement or validation.

## AI-Enabled Analytics

I use AI as an **analytical productivity layer**, not as a substitute for data analysis.

My approach includes using AI to support:

- **Analytical exploration** — generating potential hypotheses and investigation paths from business questions
- **SQL & DAX development** — accelerating query and measure development before validating logic and outputs
- **Data-quality investigation** — identifying potential anomalies, inconsistencies, and validation checks
- **Root-cause analysis** — exploring possible drivers behind unusual business performance
- **Documentation** — converting technical analysis into structured, stakeholder-friendly documentation
- **Insight communication** — improving the clarity of executive summaries and business recommendations
- **Workflow efficiency** — reducing time spent on repetitive analytical tasks so more effort can be focused on interpretation and decision support

All AI-assisted outputs are reviewed against the underlying data before being incorporated into final analysis or recommendations.

## Compensation & Pay Equity Diagnostic Analysis

Conducted an organization-wide compensation analysis covering **1,000 employees** to investigate pay equity across gender, department, seniority, education, job title, and performance.

The analysis identified a **9.5% unadjusted gender pay gap**, tested alternative explanations across organizational dimensions, identified particularly large early-career disparities, and found almost no relationship between performance ratings and compensation.

The project demonstrates the distinction between descriptive findings and causal conclusions by recommending further multivariate analysis before treating the observed gap as an adjusted pay-equity estimate.

**Tools:** Power BI | Power Query | DAX | HR Analytics

[View Project](https://github.com/Tsmjeff/compensation-pay-equity-analysis)

## How I Use Data to Support Decisions

I approach analytics as a decision-support function rather than simply a reporting exercise.

My work is structured around four questions:

**1. What happened?**  
Establish reliable KPIs, trends, and performance patterns.

**2. Why did it happen?**  
Segment performance and investigate potential drivers and root causes.

**3. Why does it matter?**  
Translate analytical findings into commercial, operational, or organizational implications.

**4. What should happen next?**  
Develop measurable, evidence-based recommendations for decision-makers.

AI helps accelerate parts of this workflow, but the final interpretation remains grounded in validated data and business context.
